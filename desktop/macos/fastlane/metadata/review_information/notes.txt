2. WHY THE APP HAS TWO MODES

Account mode syncs data through our servers and runs the AI Coach on our
own provider key. Those carry running costs, and they are what the Evolve
Pro subscription pays for.

Private mode keeps everything on the user's Mac. There are no server
costs, so every feature is free and no subscription, paywall or purchase
option appears anywhere. Because nothing is offered for sale there, the
subscription section is intentionally hidden in that mode. The
subscription is visible and purchasable only in Account mode, after
signing in.

On Guideline 5.1.1(v): registration is never required to use Evolve. All
of the app's content and features - habits, goals, full statistics, mood
check-in - are available in Private mode with no account, no personal
information and no payment; the AI Coach works there too, using the
user's own API key or a local model. An account is needed only for what
is inherently account-specific: syncing across the user's devices, and
running the AI Coach on our server-side key. Evolve Pro buys exactly
those two things.

5. com.apple.security.network.server ENTITLEMENT

This app uses the OAuth 2.0 loopback redirect pattern (RFC 8252 section
7.3) for Google sign-in. The app binds a temporary HTTP server on
127.0.0.1:39876 (localhost only) to receive the OAuth callback from the
system browser; it accepts a single request, extracts the authorization
code, and shuts down immediately. It is not a persistent network service.
Without this entitlement the App Sandbox blocks the loopback bind() and
Google sign-in fails. See auth_controller.dart, _signInWithDesktopOAuth().
