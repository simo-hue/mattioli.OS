Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about what Mattioli.OS is, how it works, and who makes it.

What is Mattioli.OS?

It's a complete operating system for personal growth. It's not just a habit tracker, but an integrated suite for managing daily habits, long-term goals and performance analysis.

Why 'OS' (Operating System)?

Because it aims to replace the fragmented infrastructure we usually use (scattered notes, multiple apps, Excel sheets) with a single centralized and consistent system to manage your life.

Is it really free?

Yes, 100%. It's an Open Source project released under the MIT license. Your data is yours, there's no premium subscription.

How is it different from Notion or Todoist?

Mattioli.OS is 'opinionated'. Notion is flexible but requires hours for setup. Todoist is focused on lists. This system is built specifically for discipline and quantitative data analysis, ready to use.

What is the core philosophy?

It's based on 'Atomic Habits' by James Clear: 'We don't rise to the level of our goals, we fall to the level of our systems.' The focus is on reducing friction and visualizing progress.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes, the system uses Supabase Auth to ensure your data is secure, encrypted and accessible only to you. The account is free.

Who is the developer?

I'm Simone Mattioli, a developer passionate about productivity and data visualization. I initially built this tool for myself.

What is the 'Daily Protocol'?

It's your list of non-negotiable daily habits. The heart of the system for building consistency.

How does the 'Tri-State' logic work?

Each habit can have 3 states: 'Done' (completed, green), 'Missed' (failed, red), 'Skipped' (legitimately skipped, gray). This offers more nuance than simple yes/no.

What's the difference between Missed and Skipped?

'Missed' penalizes your streak and statistics (you failed). 'Skipped' is neutral (e.g., you were sick or on vacation), it doesn't break the streak but doesn't contribute to the score.

How do I delete a habit?

If the habit has historical data, it gets 'archived' (Soft Delete) to not lose past statistics. If it has no data, it gets permanently deleted.

Can I customize the colors?

Absolutely. Each habit can have a specific color to help you visually group them (e.g., Health = Green, Work = Blue).

What is a 'Streak'?

It's the number of consecutive days you've completed a habit without 'Missed'. 'Skipped' keeps the streak frozen.

Can I set habits for specific days only?

Yes, you can define the frequency (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri). On other days the habit won't appear in your daily list.

Can I track numeric values?

Yes, you can enable quantitative tracking (e.g., '2 liters of water', '10 pages read') in addition to completed status.

What are Macro Goals?

These are long-term goals structured hierarchically: Yearly, Quarterly, Monthly and Weekly.

How do I visualize my long-term progress?

There's a dedicated dashboard ('Map') with radar charts, completion trends and category analysis to see if you're balancing your life well.

What is the 'Memento Mori' calendar?

A visualization of your life in weeks (from presumed birth to statistical death). It serves to create urgency and give value to time.

Can I see the history of past years?

Yes, the 'All-Time' dashboard allows you to navigate through data from all years you've used the system, with aggregated KPIs.

Is there a limit to the number of goals?

Technically no. The system handles efficient pagination even for thousands of records, but for your sanity we recommend focusing on a few priorities.

How does the AI Coach work?

It analyzes your data (habits, mood, goals) and generates weekly reports with personalized advice. It works like a virtual mental coach.

Is my data sent to OpenAI/Google?

NO. By default the system is designed to use 'Local LLM' (Ollama). Data is processed locally on your machine. Privacy first.

What does 'Local LLM' mean?

It means the artificial intelligence model (e.g., Llama 3, Mistral) runs on your computer, not on a remote server. No data leaves your network.

How do I configure Ollama?

You need to download Ollama from the official website, install a model (e.g., `ollama run llama3`) and make sure the server is active on port 11434.

Can I use the system without AI?

Certainly. The AI Coach is an optional module. If you don't configure it or disable it in settings, the rest of the app works perfectly.

Does the AI read my personal notes?

Only if you explicitly allow it to generate correlations between your mood and your performance.

Where is the data saved?

On Supabase, an open source and secure PostgreSQL database. You are the only owner of your data through authentication.

Can I make a backup?

Yes. There's a 'Complete Backup' function that downloads a ZIP archive containing all your data in JSON format, organized by tables.

Can I import/export data?

Yes, the backup system supports both complete export and import, useful for migrating or securing information.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes! Mattioli.OS is available as a native iOS app in the Apple App Store. The companion web dashboard is also fully responsive and installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on any mobile home screen.

What is the tech stack?

React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Supabase, TanStack Query, Recharts.

Can I self-host the database?

Yes, since Supabase is open source, you can host your own Docker instance if you have the technical skills to do so.

I found a bug, what do I do?

Open an issue on GitHub. The project is actively maintained and community contributions are welcome.

Why isn't the AI responding?

Verify that Ollama is running (`ollama serve`) and that the selected model is downloaded. Also check that the browser isn't blocking calls to localhost (CORS).

The site seems slow with lots of data?

We've implemented recursive pagination and virtual scrolling. If you notice slowdowns above 100k records, let us know.

How do I update to the new version?

If you use the web version, simply refresh the page. If you cloned the repo, do `git pull` and `npm run build`.
