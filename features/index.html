    Full Feature List

    More Than Just
    Checkboxes.

    A true operating system for life. Every module is designed to work in synergy with the others.

    Daily Mastery

    The heart of the system. Surgical tracking of your routines.

    Tri-State Logic

    Not just done/not done. Manage 'Done', 'Missed' and 'Skipped' (legitimate) for real statistics.

    Frequency Control

    Set habits for specific days (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri) or daily.

    Quantifiable Goals

    Track precise numbers: '2 liters of water', '10 pages read', '45 minutes workout'.

    Streak Freeze

    'Skipped' mode freezes your streak without breaking it when you're sick or on vacation.

    Long-Term Vision

    Because discipline without direction is useless.

    Macro Goals

    Yearly, Quarterly and Monthly goals connected to your daily actions.

    Life View

    The 'Memento Mori' visualization: your entire life in months, from 2003 to 2088. Impactful.

    Yearly Trends

    Comparative analysis of past years. Discover which was your most productive year.

    Category Balance

    Radar chart to see if you're neglecting vital areas (Health, Work, Relationships).

    Intelligence & AI

    Your second brain, powered by local AI.

    AI Neural Coach

    Uses Local LLM (Ollama) to analyze your data and give you customized weekly advice.

    Mood & Energy Matrix

    Track Energy and Mood on a Cartesian plane. Discover when you're at peak performance.

    Privacy First AI

    The AI runs on YOUR computer. No data is sent to external servers or cloud AI.

    Correlations

    The system learns: 'When you sleep poorly (Mood), you often skip workouts (Habit)'.

    Utilities Suite

    All the tools you need, in one place.

    Reading Tracker

    Specific calendar for reading. Track pages and daily consistency.

    Markdown Memos

    Integrated text editor with Markdown support for quick notes and ideas.

    Smart Deletion

    Intelligent archiving (Soft Delete) to not lose the history of deleted habits.

    Full Backup

    Export all your data in JSON with one click. You own your information.

    Mobile & Desktop Native

    All these features are 100% accessible from any device. The interface adapts fluidly from your desktop's 4K monitor to your smartphone screen.
