Evolve is the ultimate personal growth tool designed for those who want to build solid habits, set clear goals, and deeply understand their behavior patterns through scientific and distraction-free data analysis.

Unlike traditional productivity applications, which are often chaotic and overloaded with notifications or ads, Evolve is based on a minimal interface, an extremely refined design, and an advanced analytics engine. The application helps you understand not only if you are completing your tasks, but how your habits influence each other and how to best balance your well-being with your daily productivity.

Evolve's functional macro-areas:

1. ADVANCED HABIT TRACKING
- Interactive and Actionable Notifications: Log the completion of a habit directly from the system notification, without having to open the app.
- Single and Flexible Reminders: Set independent notification times for each habit.
- Apple-Style Customization: Configure your milestones visually.
- Dynamic Streak Calculation: Monitor your consistency.
- Historical Calendar: View the monthly and yearly progress of every single habit to accurately identify your consistency over time.

2. GOAL DEFINITION AND DEVELOPMENT
- Sub-Goal Breakdown: Break down your macro-goals into small, achievable steps.
- Radar Charts for Life Areas: Visualize the overall balance of your growth through charts.
- Seasonality and Historical Analysis: Observe how your productivity varies across different months of the year and adapt your rhythms accordingly.
- Yearly Trend: Track your long-term progress to keep your focus high on your macro-milestones.

3. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS AND CORRELATIONS
Evolve transforms your daily actions into useful data for your mindful growth.
- Wellness vs Output Index: A proprietary algorithm relates your emotional well-being to your actual productivity, helping you find the perfect balance to prevent burnout.
- Correlation Analysis: Discover how one habit influences another. For example, analyze how sleep quality or physical activity directly affects your daytime focus.
- Identification of Best and Critical Habits: The app automatically recognizes which behaviors are supporting your growth and which require immediate support.

4. MINDFULNESS
Personal growth is not just made of numbers, but also of mental awareness.
- Mood Check-In: Log your emotional state to understand the factors influencing your productivity.
- Life View: A visual widget based on your birth date representing the weeks of your life passed and those to come. A powerful tool to remind you of the value of time and push you to live every day with intention.
- Secure Cloud Synchronization: All your data is saved in real-time securely thanks to Supabase integration and Apple Sign-In authentication.
- Consistent Premium Design: Switch seamlessly from Dark Mode to Light Mode with a design tailored for maximum readability and visual comfort in any lighting condition.
