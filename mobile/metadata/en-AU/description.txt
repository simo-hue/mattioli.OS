Evolve is the ultimate personal growth tool designed for those who want to build solid habits, set clear goals, and deeply understand their behavior patterns through scientific, distraction-free data analysis.

Unlike traditional productivity apps, which are often chaotic and overloaded with notifications or ads, Evolve is based on a minimalist interface, a highly curated design, and an advanced analytics engine. The app helps you understand not only if you are completing your tasks, but how your habits influence each other and how to best balance your well-being with your daily productivity.

Evolve's macro-functional areas:

1. ADVANCED HABIT TRACKING
- Interactive and Actionable Notifications: Record habit completion directly from the system notification, without opening the app.
- Individual and Flexible Reminders: Set independent notification times for each habit.
- Apple-Style Customization: Visually configure your milestones.
- Dynamic Streak Calculation: Track your consistency.
- Historical Calendar: View the monthly and yearly progress of each individual habit to precisely identify your consistency over time.

2. GOAL SETTING AND DEVELOPMENT
- Breakdown into Sub-Goals: Divide your macro-goals into small, achievable steps.
- Radar Charts for Life Areas: Visualize the overall balance of your growth through charts.
- Seasonality Analysis and History: Observe how your productivity varies in different months of the year and adjust your pace accordingly.
- Yearly Trend: Track long-term progress to stay focused on your macro-goals.

3. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS AND CORRELATIONS
Evolve transforms your daily actions into useful data for your mindful growth.
- Wellness vs Output Index: A proprietary algorithm relates your emotional well-being to your actual productivity, helping you find the perfect balance to prevent burnout.
- Correlation Analysis: Discover how one habit influences another. For example, analyze how sleep quality or physical activity directly affects your daytime focus.
- Identification of Best and Critical Habits: The app automatically recognizes which behaviors are supporting your growth and which need immediate support.

4. MINDFULNESS
Personal growth isn't just about numbers, but also mental awareness.
- Mood Check-In: Record your emotional state to understand the factors influencing your productivity.
- Life View: A visual widget based on your birth date that represents past and future weeks of life. A powerful tool to remind you of the value of time and push you to live every day with intention.
- Secure Cloud Sync: All your data is saved in real-time securely thanks to integration with Supabase and Apple Sign-In authentication.
- Coherent Premium Design: Seamlessly switch from Dark Mode to Light Mode thanks to a design studied for maximum readability and visual comfort in any lighting condition.
