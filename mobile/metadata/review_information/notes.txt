1) PRIVACY AND DATA USE (GUIDELINE 5.1.2)

We are adding this section proactively. Our macOS app, which shares this bundle ID, was recently asked under Guideline 5.1.2 to confirm what it uploads to a server. The answer for iOS is below in full, so the same question does not need to be asked twice.

CONTACTS, CALENDARS, MICROPHONE, LOCATION

Evolve does not access any of them and never has. There is no NSContactsUsageDescription, NSCalendarsUsageDescription, NSMicrophoneUsageDescription or NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription key in the app's Info.plist, no matching entitlement, and no code that calls CNContactStore, EKEventStore or CoreLocation. Nothing from any of those sources is uploaded, because nothing from them is read.

WHAT THE APP DOES ACCESS, AND WHY

- Apple Health (NSHealthShareUsageDescription), READ ONLY. Used to tick off a habit automatically - "10,000 steps", "7 hours of sleep" - instead of making the user check it off by hand. Only for habits the user has explicitly configured with a Health rule.
- Screen Time / DeviceActivity (family-controls entitlement), READ ONLY. The same thing for habits like "under 2 hours of social media". The DeviceActivity extension is notified when a threshold the user set is crossed; it does not receive or store usage totals.
- Camera and Photo Library. Only to let the user pick a profile picture, only when they tap the picker.
- Face ID. Only to unlock the app, if the user turns on app lock.
- Notifications. Habit reminders and the daily brief.

THE HEALTH AND SCREEN TIME BOUNDARY - THE IMPORTANT PART

Health and Screen Time measurements are read on the device and stay there. When a rule is evaluated, the only thing that can be uploaded is the resulting verdict - the habit was met, or it was not - and never the measured quantity behind it. In the code, the health-derived value is written as an explicit null into the upload payload before it is sent, and a habit whose rule cannot be resolved is treated as health-derived, so the guard fails closed rather than open.

In our Private mode the measured value may be kept, but only in the SQLCipher-encrypted database on the device and, if the user turns on iCloud sync, end-to-end encrypted inside their own iCloud account. It never reaches our servers in either mode.

CONSENT IS TAKEN BEFORE ANYTHING IS UPLOADED

The first screen on a fresh launch, before any account exists, is a full-screen privacy gate that cannot be skipped - Continue stays disabled until the user ticks the acceptance box. In this build it states in plain language, in all five app languages, exactly what leaves the device:

 - "With an Evolve account - goals, habits, mood check-ins, your app settings and your profile (name, email, date of birth) are uploaded to Evolve's servers to sync your devices. Your profile picture stays on this device."
 - "Privately on this device - nothing is uploaded to us; optional iCloud sync is end-to-end encrypted and reaches only your own iCloud account."
 - "Health and Screen Time - read on this device only, to tick off habits automatically. The measurement itself never reaches our servers - only whether the habit was met."
 - "Never accessed - your contacts, calendar, microphone or location."

The acceptance box reads "I have read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, I am at least 14 years old, and I agree to the upload described above." Both documents are linked on that same screen.

The profile picture is worth stating explicitly because it is the one thing users expect to be uploaded and is not: the app makes no object-storage call anywhere. A picture the user chooses is written to a local directory, and in Private mode may travel end-to-end encrypted through their own iCloud. It never reaches our servers.

THE THREE OTHER TRANSMISSIONS

- Crash diagnostics (Sentry). An explicit opt-in switch on that same privacy gate, OFF by default. The SDK cannot start before the user has answered the question, answering "no" - the default - keeps it off permanently, it can be withdrawn at any time in Settings, and it never runs at all in Private mode.
- AI Coach. Sending a conversation requires its own separate consent dialog naming who receives it, shown before the first message; dismissing it counts as refusal. If the user runs a local or own-key model, nothing leaves the device and no consent is asked, because no transmission occurs.
- Subscription management (RevenueCat). In Account mode only, and only once the subscription screen is opened or a purchase restored, our payments provider receives the account's user id and the App Store purchase state. No habit, goal, mood, health or profile data is sent.


2) GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) - NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED

Evolve does not require registration to reach any content or feature.

The first screen after the consent step asks how you want to use Evolve and offers two equal choices. The first is "Continue without an account". Tapping it opens the app immediately, with no sign-up, no email address and no personal information of any kind.

Used this way, every feature that runs on the device is unlocked and free:
- unlimited habits and unlimited goals (no caps at all)
- the full statistics engine, including deep charts and trend analysis
- Apple Health and Screen Time habit verification
- the AI Coach, using an OpenRouter key you provide yourself

There is nothing to purchase on this path. To confirm that, open Profile > Subscription while using the app without an account: the screen states that every Evolve Pro feature is already included at no cost, and offers no purchase control.

We apologise that our previous notes sent you straight to a test account and never mentioned this path. That was our mistake, and it is why the review reasonably concluded registration was required.

Why the subscription exists at all: everything that runs on the device is free because it costs us nothing to operate. Evolve Pro covers only what we run on our own servers for the user - syncing across their devices, and a hosted AI Coach that needs no API key of their own. Both are account-based by nature, since there is no account to sync or to authenticate without one.


3) GUIDELINE 3.1.2(c) - TERMS OF USE (EULA)

We use Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA), linked in the App Description.

You were right that this was missing, and reviewing our own listing we found the problem was worse than we first understood. Two separate faults:

- English (U.S.) and Spanish (Spain) were showing the description of an entirely different product of ours. That is our error and we are sorry you were shown it; the correct Evolve description is in this submission for both.
- German, French, Canadian French and Greek had been truncated at the 4000-character description limit, and because the legal links sat at the end of the text the truncation removed them. Mexican Spanish kept the Terms link but lost the end of the privacy URL.

We have rebuilt how these descriptions are produced. The text is translated first and the legal links are composed afterwards from fixed URLs, so they never pass through translation and are budgeted to always fit — they can no longer be truncated. All 39 localisations are now validated automatically before upload. Every localisation in this submission carries both links.

Inside the app, the subscription screen shows the plan name, the duration, the price and the price per month from StoreKit, the renewal terms, and functional links to both the Privacy Policy and Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA).

These links are also reachable at any time, in every mode and whether or not the user subscribes, from Settings > Legal.
